About us

About us

Why was Mrunvedaa Foundation started?

For six months, a mother saved every spare coin in a small tin jar. The goal was simple: a new school uniform and books for her daughter. It was a ticket to a better life. But in a single afternoon, that dream vanished. Her son developed a severe respiratory infection and at the pharmacy counter, she was forced into an impossible choice. The cost of the life-saving inhaler was, to the cent, the exact amount saved for school. She bought the medicine. Her son breathed again, but her daughter’s education stopped that day.

This is why we started this foundation. Poverty is more than a lack of money; it is a series of heartbreaking choices between a child’s breath and a child’s future. We provide the healthcare safety net that protects a family’s savings, ensuring that medical crises never derail an education. We bridge the gap between health and learning—because no parent should ever have to choose between a prescription and a pen.

Our Vision

A world where a family's future is never sidelined by a medical bill.

Our Mission
